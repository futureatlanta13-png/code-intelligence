Google Workspace integration

Connect with purpose.
Stay in control.

Code Intelligence can integrate with supported Google Workspace services when you explicitly connect your Google account.

  1. You choose to connect

    Connecting your account is voluntary. You review the requested permissions before authorizing access.

  2. Data serves your workflow

    Depending on the workflow, the application may access files, documents or spreadsheets you choose to use. Access is used only to provide the functionality you request.

  3. You can revoke access

    You remain in control of your permissions and can remove access at any time in your Google Account settings.
